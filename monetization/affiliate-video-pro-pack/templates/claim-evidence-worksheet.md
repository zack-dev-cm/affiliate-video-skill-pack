# Claim Evidence Worksheet

| Claim | Risk | Evidence URL or note | Safer wording | Approved |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  | low / medium / high |  |  | no |

## Rewrite Rules

- Replace guarantees with bounded language.
- Remove first-person results unless the operator personally used the product.
- Do not imply disease treatment, cure, diagnosis, prevention, guaranteed income, or typical transformation.
- Keep evidence close to the exact claim. Product-page marketing text is not automatically enough for health, finance, or safety claims.

## Examples

| Risky | Safer |
| --- | --- |
| This fixed my sleep. | A simple bedtime routine idea featuring this product. |
| Guaranteed passive income. | A workflow template for tracking affiliate campaigns. |
| Doctor recommended. | Check the product page and speak to a qualified professional for medical questions. |
