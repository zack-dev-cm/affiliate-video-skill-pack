## Decision

- `Proceed`: 12+ with no high-risk claims.
- `Manual review`: 8-11 or any sensitive category.
- `Reject`: below 8, unsupported health/income claims, or unclear rights.

## Safer First Niches

- Creator desk gear
- Camera/mobile accessories
- Home organization
- Kitchen workflow tools
- Travel organization
- Productivity software with demonstrable features
