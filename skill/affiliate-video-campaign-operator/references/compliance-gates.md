# Compliance Gates

This is a practical pre-publish checklist, not legal advice. Check current program and platform rules before publishing.

## Disclosure Gate

Block publishing until:

- The post, caption, landing page, or video clearly says the creator may earn money or that the link is paid.
- Video endorsements include disclosure inside the video, not only in the description.
- Carousel endorsements include disclosure on the CTA slide and caption.
- The disclosure appears before or near the affiliate link or recommendation.
- The platform's native paid partnership or commercial content label is used when available and required.
- Amazon Associates campaigns include the required Associate identity statement where Amazon authorizes use of program content.

Good short examples:

- `#ad`
- `Paid link`
- `I may earn a commission if you buy through this link.`
- `As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.`

Avoid vague wording:

- `affiliate link` by itself
- `collab`
- `sp`
- disclosures buried in hashtags
- disclosures only on profile/about pages

## Claim Gate

Block or rewrite:

- disease, cure, treatment, diagnosis, or prevention claims
- guaranteed transformation claims
- income promises
- safety claims without standards evidence
- before/after results that imply typical outcomes without proof
- personal-use claims when the operator has not used the product

Use safer wording:

- `designed for`
- `may help with`
- `routine idea`
- `check the product details and reviews`
- `talk to a qualified professional for medical questions`

## Sensitive Product Gate

Manual review required for:

- supplements, vitamins, sleep aids, anxiety or stress products
- skincare that claims medical or therapeutic effects
- weight loss or body transformation products
- finance, investing, crypto, credit, insurance
- baby products, safety devices, medical devices
- regulated goods or age-restricted goods
- "make money" offers

For TikTok branded content, be especially conservative with healthcare, pharmaceuticals, supplements, weight loss, finance, gambling, adult products, and other prohibited or restricted categories.

## Platform Gate

Pinterest:

- Affiliate content should be original and add unique value.
- Disclose commercial nature and use available paid partnership tools when applicable.
- Avoid repetitive high-volume pinning, fake accounts, quid-pro-quo saves, and misleading redirects.

TikTok:

- Affiliate commission content is branded content.
- Use the commercial content disclosure toggle when required.
- Identify the promoted product clearly.
- Do not publish false, misleading, subliminal, exploitative, or prohibited-category content.

Instagram:

- Use the paid partnership label for affiliate-link posts/reels when required.
- Keep disclosures visible and avoid relying only on hashtags after the fold.
- Use original or meaningfully created content.

YouTube:

- Select the paid promotion box when the video contains paid product placement, endorsement, sponsorship, or other required disclosure.
- Keep affiliate disclosure in the video and description for Shorts or long-form reviews.

## Asset Rights Gate

Record:

- product image source
- permission or license note
- generated asset provider and model
- prompt file or prompt summary
- final asset path
- music source and rights note
- landing page screenshot or product page snapshot when claims rely on it
